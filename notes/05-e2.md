## E2-freetext: how to phrase free-text answers ($0.029)
Variants (prompt rule for fields typed freetext by stage 0), 4 single-entry docs, everything else cached:
- **describe** (current): the field's description decides the phrasing.
- **verbatim**: copy the shortest passage that states the term, word for word.
- **concise**: answer in the length and format of the description's example, in the document's words, keeping every fact.

Scored strictly (`tools/validate.py`) and with a judge from another model family (claude-haiku-5.5; 47 distinct pairs,
2 calls, $0.0016). Spot check: the judge is too lenient on short identifier fields (accepted "Second Floor, West
Section, Suite 214W" for "Suite 214W"), so the realistic judged score applies the judge only to fields typed freetext,
matching the brief ("slightly more lenient on free-text fields").

| Variant | Strict | Judged (freetext only) | Judged (all text) | $ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| describe | 0.845 | 0.889 | 0.902 | 0 (cached) |
| verbatim | 0.835 | **0.928** | 0.950 | 0.0137 |
| concise | **0.858** | 0.914 | 0.935 | 0.0137 |

Differences are near the ±0.03 noise on 4 documents. **concise** is best under strict scoring and within 0.014 of the
best judged score; **verbatim** is best judged but worst strict. Decision pending (user).

**Revision (user):** keep both verbatim and concise available (`FREETEXT_MODE`); note the judge's leniency on
identifier fields. Default set to **concise**.

## Improvement round 1 (A: table checks, D: citations) — $0.025

**A. Table plans.** Two generic checks: a `value` block field that matches several differing lines in >1% of records
(should it be `sum`?), and section header rows may lack the key column (prompt). Re-planning exposed **plan
instability**: the new luna plan read the rent-roll total from the unit's first line (0.946). Tried:
- other plan models: glm (0.943 mean over the 4 tables) and haiku (0.947) each fail a *different* table
  (both drop the T12's TOTAL rows that carry GL codes);
- majority vote over the 3 plans ($0, offline): fixes the rent roll but breaks the T12 (the two wrong plans agree), 0.951;
- **cross-field checks** written by the plan model from descriptions ("should equal rent plus other charges"),
  verified by code on every row. Optional checks were ignored; made the `checks` key **required** (empty list
  allowed) → the plan wrote the check, it failed on the misread total, the repair fixed it.

Result: boxscore 1.000, budget 0.999, t12 0.997, rentroll **1.000** (tables mean 0.999).

**D. Citations ($0, replay).** Header values in PDF-table grids now cite pages (budget: 741/741 values with
