## E2-freetext: how to phrase free-text answers ($0.029)
Variants (prompt rule for fields typed freetext by stage 0), 4 single-entry docs, everything else cached:
- **describe** (current): the field's description decides the phrasing.
- **verbatim**: copy the shortest passage that states the term, word for word.
- **concise**: answer in the length and format of the description's example, in the document's words, keeping every fact.

Scored strictly (`tools/validate.py`) and with a judge from another model family (claude-haiku-5.5; 47 distinct pairs,
2 calls, $0.0016). Spot check: the judge is too lenient on short identifier fields (accepted "Second Floor, West
Section, Suite 214W" for "Suite 214W"), so the realistic judged score applies the judge only to fields typed freetext,
matching the brief ("slightly more lenient on free-text fields").
